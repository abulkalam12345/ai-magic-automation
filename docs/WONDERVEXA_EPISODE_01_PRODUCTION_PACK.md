## Story

A magician makes a lemon vanish. Her reflection then stops copying her, smiles independently, and steps out of the mirror. The original woman disappears. The double takes her place and looks into camera.

**Opening hook:** SHE VANISHED. HER REFLECTION DIDN’T.  
**Ending question:** WHO REALLY ESCAPED THE MIRROR?

## Shot list and dialogue

### Shot 1 — 00:00–00:03: Eye-contact hook
**Visual:** Extreme close-up of the magician’s eyes in a dim elegant room. She looks straight into the lens; one sharp catchlight; slow micro push-in.

**Hindi VO:** “रुको… अपनी आँखें मत झपकाना।”  
**English subtitle:** “Wait… don't blink.”  
**Delivery:** Intimate urgent whisper; start immediately.

### Shot 2 — 00:03–00:07: Lemon vanish
**Visual:** Medium close-up. She shows one realistic yellow lemon clearly in her right hand, closes her fingers, then opens an empty palm. The lemon vanishes cleanly. Keep camera locked so the trick reads.

**Hindi VO:** “ये सिर्फ़ एक नींबू नहीं… मेरा पहला जादू है।”  
**English subtitle:** “This isn't just a lemon… it's my first trick.”  
**Delivery:** Playful confidence; pause after “नहीं”; hit the vanish on “जादू”.

### Shot 3 — 00:07–00:12: Mirror glitch
**Visual:** Over-shoulder view of a tall antique mirror. The real woman remains still; her reflection lags by half a second, then smiles independently.

**Hindi VO:** “लेकिन… आईने में कुछ गड़बड़ है।”  
**English subtitle:** “But… something is wrong with the mirror.”  
**Delivery:** Lower cautious tone; suspense pause after “लेकिन”.

### Shot 4 — 00:12–00:18: Reflection disobeys
**Visual:** Symmetrical medium shot showing both the woman and her reflection. Real woman keeps hands down. Reflection independently raises one hand and touches the glass from inside. The real woman reacts.

**Hindi VO:** “मेरी परछाईं… मेरी नकल करना बंद कर चुकी है!”  
**English subtitle:** “My reflection… has stopped copying me!”  
**Delivery:** Build tension; stress “परछाईं”.

### Shot 5 — 00:18–00:24: Double escapes
**Visual:** Mirror ripples like liquid. An identical double steps naturally through the mirror. At the same moment, the original vanishes in a brief dark-violet particle burst.

**Hindi VO:** “नहीं! ये क्या… मेरी जगह कौन आ रहा है?!”  
**English subtitle:** “No! What's happening… who's taking my place?!”  
**Delivery:** Sudden startled energy on “नहीं!”, then urgent and breathy.

### Shot 6 — 00:24–00:30: Final twist
**Visual:** The escaped double stands alone, faces camera and gives a small chilling smile. Hold the look, then cut to black at 00:30.

**Hindi VO:** “अब… इस दुनिया में मेरी बारी है।”  
**English subtitle:** “Now… it's my turn in this world.”  
**Delivery:** Slow controlled whisper; cold confidence on “मेरी बारी”; final silence.
