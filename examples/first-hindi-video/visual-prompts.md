# Visual prompts — Can Time Be Stopped?

**Canvas:** 9:16 vertical  
**Target:** 40–45 seconds total  
**Look:** Cinematic science-fiction realism, deep navy and electric blue  
**Status:** Prompt pack only. No footage has been generated.

## Continuity lock

Use one consistent fictional adult protagonist throughout: same face, dark jacket, hairstyle, and proportions. Keep the same modern city street, architecture, vehicles, weather, and blue-hour lighting. Preserve realistic scale and screen direction. Do not include recognizable real people, brands, copyrighted characters, text, logos, or watermarks in generated footage.

## Shot 1 — The instant freeze (0–5s)

Vertical cinematic wide shot of a busy modern city street at blue hour. Traffic and pedestrians are in motion for a brief moment, then everything freezes at once. A single raindrop hangs in the air. Smooth controlled camera push-in, realistic lighting, deep navy palette with subtle electric-blue reflections.

## Shot 2 — A world held still (5–12s)

Continue in the identical street and lighting. Show a bird suspended mid-flight, a water droplet hanging above a puddle, and vehicles completely motionless. Slow lateral camera move. Frozen objects remain stable, with no flicker or accidental motion.

## Shot 3 — The only moving person (12–20s)

The same protagonist cautiously walks through the frozen street while every other person and object remains still. Keep character design, jacket, street layout, and camera direction consistent. Subtle blue rim light separates the protagonist from the still world.

## Shot 4 — Questions, not claims (20–29s)

Cinematic close-ups: the protagonist looks at their hand, then at a wristwatch; a visible breath cloud appears in cold air. Use abstract heartbeat-like light pulses only as visual metaphors. Do not imply these visuals prove what would happen in real physics.

## Shot 5 — Thought experiment reveal (29–37s)

A vast abstract clock face and suspended particles in a dark blue cosmic space. The hands are motionless. Camera slowly pulls back to reveal the scene as a speculative visual concept. Keep the image elegant and uncluttered.

## Shot 6 — WonderVexa end frame (37–45s)

Return to the same protagonist facing a luminous electric-blue circular portal on the city street. Hold a clean composition with lower-right safe space for the separate WonderVexa watermark and central safe space for the final audience question.

## Negative prompt

Landscape frame, low resolution, inconsistent protagonist, changing wardrobe, changing city, duplicate people, extra limbs, warped faces, flicker, jitter, strobing, chaotic camera movement, unreadable text, logos, signatures, watermarks, copyrighted characters, recognizable film scenes, imitation of a living artist.
