# WonderVexa first proof: Wilted Flower Restoration

**Status:** Creative draft only — not generated, rendered, or approved for publication  
**Target:** 10 seconds, vertical 9:16 (1080x1920 final export)  
**Purpose:** A small, reviewable proof of the visual direction before choosing any video provider.

## Creative premise

A single wilted bluebell-like flower stands in a quiet, moonlit garden. A soft blue-and-gold magical ring appears around it. The flower slowly regains its color and opens into a luminous bloom. The magic is gentle and readable; the flower remains the same plant throughout.

## Generation prompt

Create an original cinematic fantasy macro shot in vertical 9:16. One single delicate wilted bluebell-like flower grows from a small patch of dark garden soil at night. The flower has a slightly bent green stem and folded, dry blue-violet petals. A restrained circular ribbon of blue and warm gold magical light gently orbits it once. Tiny luminous particles travel from the light to the flower. In one continuous, physically readable transformation, the stem straightens, the petals regain rich blue-violet color, and the flower opens into a healthy elegant glowing bloom. Moonlit garden bokeh in the background, shallow depth of field, polished high-detail 3D fantasy realism, elegant lighting, coherent botanical shape, stable subject identity, gentle motion, emotionally uplifting reveal. Keep the flower centered and fully visible, with clean lower-right negative space for a later watermark. No text, no logos, no watermark baked into the generated footage.

## Negative prompt / avoid

Multiple flowers, extra stems, changing flower species, duplicated petals, deformed plant anatomy, hands, people, faces, sudden jump cuts, fast camera spins, chaotic particles, explosions, fire, excessive bloom glare, flicker, strobing, unreadable text, logos, signatures, baked-in watermark, borders, landscape framing.

## Edit and audio notes

- Preserve the continuous transformation; trim only dead time, not the reveal.
- Use silence or audio that is demonstrably licensed for the intended platforms.
- Keep music and effects low enough that the visual remains clear.
- Add the WonderVexa watermark during editing, not inside the generated footage.
- Do not add platform-specific UI, calls to action, or subtitles over the flower.
- Keep a clean master without platform-specific end cards.
